> AI and process-automation services that help businesses reduce repetitive work, connect their tools and build reliable workflows. Websites and other technical services are secondary options.

> I help businesses turn repetitive processes into useful, maintainable workflows. Projects can include AI-assisted document handling, intake and follow-up, connecting existing business tools, and small custom applications. I also build business websites and help improve existing technical systems. Each project starts with the problem, the data it needs and where human review should remain.
